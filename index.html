01.About me

A bit about who I am and how I work.

I'm a Full Stack Developer at Grassroots in Bangalore, where I build and ship features end-to-end on a React.js/Node.js stack for a production user base. I design the RESTful APIs behind business-critical workflows, own database schema design and query optimization, and help streamline our CI/CD pipelines so releases move faster with fewer manual steps.

Before that I was a Front-End Developer Intern at Enhancio, building reusable React components, redesigning core UI/UX flows with designers, and fixing cross-browser rendering issues across Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.

Academically, I hold a Bachelor of Computer Applications from Dayananda Sagar University and completed one year of coursework toward a Master of Data Science at Monash University (degree not conferred), covering machine learning, AI, statistical modelling and data analytics.

Outside of work I prefer building whole systems over isolated exercises: real-time chat, microservices, NLP pipelines, and chess analytics, each with tests and a reproducible benchmark or evaluation.
